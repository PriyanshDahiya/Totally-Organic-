Questions

Fair questions.

Is this AI avatars or fake influencers?

No. No AI people, no synthetic video. Posts are text overlays on real stock footage or your own clips and product photos: the format that already works for brands on Reels.

Do you repost other creators' videos?

Never. We borrow the structure of trending hooks (the "POV:", the "what people think I do vs what I do"), not anyone's video. Footage comes from licensed stock or your own media.

What's a credit?

One credit is one finished, downloadable video. Browsing the feed, editing text, swapping footage and regenerating ideas are all free. If a render ever fails, the credit comes back automatically.

Will it know my brand's tone?

It reads your website to start, and you can edit everything: your customers, their pain points, your tone do's and don'ts, even example posts you love. It also learns from what you swipe.

Does it post to Instagram for me?

Auto-posting to Instagram is coming with launch. During early access you download the videos and post them yourself (it takes seconds).

Who is this for?

Indian D2C and Shopify brands that know they should be posting Reels every day but don't have a content team: skincare, grooming, food, wellness, fashion, home and pet brands.
